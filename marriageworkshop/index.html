Meet your facilitator

Nellie Reedy

Marriage Education Facilitator
Master's Candidate in Couple and Family Therapy at UNLV · Graduating December 2026

Nellie earned her bachelor's degree in psychology from UNLV and is currently completing her master's degree in Couple and Family Therapy at UNLV. She has completed more than 400 hours of supervised clinical training working with individuals, couples, and families.

Her education and clinical study have included the Gottman Method, Sue Johnson's Emotionally Focused Therapy, and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. Through Rekindle, Nellie brings this research-informed foundation into a warm, practical learning environment designed to help couples communicate more effectively, deepen connection, and develop healthier relationship patterns.

Nellie is also a wife and mother of five. She understands firsthand how parenting, demanding schedules, and the pressures of everyday life can make it difficult for couples to prioritize their relationship, and how meaningful it can be when they intentionally reconnect.

Good questions

Before you reserve

Do we have to be struggling to join?

Not at all. Rekindle is built for couples who are doing okay and want to be great, as much as for couples who feel stuck. It is proactive by design.

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. Rekindle is marriage education and guided skill-building in a small-group setting. It is not therapy, counseling, clinical treatment, or diagnosis. Your facilitator is a Marriage Education Facilitator and Master's Candidate in Couple and Family Therapy, not a licensed therapist. If deeper or clinical needs surface, we will gladly help you find the right licensed professional.

How long is it?

It is a six-week workshop, with one session a week. Each session is 75 minutes of teaching plus discussion and activities, with light assignments between sessions. Evening, weekend, live, and online options are available.

What does it cost, and do we both attend?

It is $600 per couple for the full program, and yes, you attend together. That covers every session, your at-home toolkit, and your invitation to the six-month follow-up.

What happens after we reserve?

You get an instant confirmation, then pay on this page. After payment, we send your cohort schedule and welcome details.

Is any of this refundable?

Yes. If you are unable to attend, your payment is refunded minus a $35 processing fee.
