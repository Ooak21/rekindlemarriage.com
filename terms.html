Who the program is for

Rekindle is for adult couples, aged 18 or over, who want to strengthen a relationship they intend to keep. Both partners attend together. Rekindle is not an appropriate setting for a relationship involving abuse, and it is not crisis care.

If you are in danger right now, do not use this website to get help. Call 911. For domestic violence, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 800-799-7233, available 24 hours a day. For a mental health or suicide crisis, call or text 988.
